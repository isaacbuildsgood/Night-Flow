# Status Report: NightFlow Productivity App

## ✅ What's Been Done
The application has been transformed into a professional-grade study companion with the following features:

### 1. Advanced Timer System
- **Dual Input:** Support for both Minutes and Seconds for precise control.
- **Pomodoro Presets:** Quick-select pills for **25m Focus**, **5m Break**, and **50m Deep Work**.
- **Auto-Transition:** Automatically starts a 5-minute break session after a 25-minute focus period.
- **Persistent Alarm:** A looping beep sounds at the end of every session until dismissed.

### 2. Focus & Anti-Distraction
- **Zen Mode:** The timer automatically pauses and alerts you if you switch tabs or minimize the window during a session.
- **Focus Goal:** A dedicated "What are you studying?" field that locks in during your session to keep you on track.
- **Motivational Quotes:** Dynamic quotes that refresh with every session to keep you inspired.

### 3. Personalization & Gamification
- **Custom Themes:** Persistent theme picker with Indigo, Rose, Emerald, and Amber options.
- **Daily Streaks:** A "🔥 Day Streak" counter that tracks consecutive study days.
- **Progress Tracking:** Real-time progress bar and a daily total of minutes studied.
- **Statistics Page:** A separate view (`stats.html`) that reflects your streak and theme.

### 4. Ambient Experience
- **Focus Sounds:** High-quality soundscapes (Rain, Fireplace, Crickets) available via a premium dropdown menu.

---

## ⏳ What Remains? (Potential Next Steps)
Since all initial goals have been completed, here are some recommended features to take the app even further:

1. **Detailed Session History:** A log of exactly what you studied each day and for how long.
2. **Task Checklist:** A mini to-do list inside the focus card to check off sub-tasks.
3. **Browser Notifications:** Receive a desktop notification when your timer ends.
4. **Volume Control:** A slider to adjust ambient sound volume independent of system volume.
5. **Sound Timer:** Option to fade out sounds as the focus session ends.
